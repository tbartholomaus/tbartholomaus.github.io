Media coverage

The following include highlights of press and other multimedia coverage of my research and teaching.

A long-form, general interest interview on the podcast Profoundly Pointless, with associated video.  We talk glacier change, sea level rise, climate change, plate tectonics, the color of ice, iceberg calving, and other topics. December 6, 2023 (audio, video)

An episode of the University of Idaho's The Vandal Theory podcast, regarding my research on glacier surges, sea level projection, and glacier moss balls. Linked here via Apple Podcasts. November 14, 2022 (audio)

Coverage of the first successful field work at Turner Glacier, as part of an NSF-funded research to study glacier surges, by partner organization Boise State University, October 22, 2020 (text).

Our paper on the mysterious and charismatics life histories of glacier moss balls (Hotaling et al., 2020), received extraordinary international coverage.  Highlights include:

A tweet that was seen by 110,000 people, and received 780 likes and 300 retweets (online).

An on-air interview with Bartholomaus on National Public Radio's Morning Edition program, May 22, 2020 (radio).

An in-depth article in Popular Science, May 28, 2020 (print).

An interview on the CBC (Canadian Broadcast Corporation) flagship radio variety show, As It Happens, starting at starting at 39:05 in the recording, May 26, 2020 (radio).

An interview on the radio show of Dublin, Ireland's Sean Moncrieff, May 28, 2020 (radio).

An 11-minute, deep-dive podcast on Shortwave, NPR's daily science podcast, featuring interviews with Bartholomaus and co-authors, June 3, 2020 (audio).

A video produced by the Weather Channel , August 7, 2020 (video).

An article in The Times (of London), June 8, 2020 (print).

A human-interest piece on the relationship between co-authors Gilbert and Bartholomaus, that ran prominently in the Anchorage Daily News, Fairbanks Daily News Miner, and other organizations that run Ned Rozell's syndicated column, June 6, 2020 (print).

An outreach piece for younger learners in Science News for Students, with original interview, July 30, 2020 (text).

An article in News for Kids, June 12, 2020 (text)

An article in The Smithsonian Magazine, June 2, 2020 (text).

A review in IFL Science (online).

A perspective in The Conversation, June 8, 2020 (online).

A review in Atlas Obscura, December 7, 2023 (online).

Commentary on an unusually shaped iceberg photographed off the coast of the Antarctic Peninsula, NBC News, October 23, 2018 (text).

An on-air interview with Bartholomaus and coverage through Boise State Public Radio, April 24, 2017 (audio)

An excellent summary article in the regional weekly newspaper, the Inlander, based on an extensive interview with Bartholomaus (print).
