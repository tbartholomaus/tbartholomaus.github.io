Photography from the Uummannaq Fjords, West Greenland

The following are photographs from 2013-2015 summer field work in the Uummannaq Fjords in West Greenland.  This work was targeted at understanding the contrasting behavior of neighboring glaciers to ocean and atmospheric forcings and was funded by NASA.  As a University of Texas Institute for Geophysics scientist, I worked with other UTIG and University of Kansas researchers and graduate students to carry out these field campaigns.
