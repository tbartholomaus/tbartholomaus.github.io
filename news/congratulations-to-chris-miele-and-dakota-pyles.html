Congratulations to Chris Miele and Dakota Pyles!

October 18, 2022

We're so proud of our two most recent lab group graduates, who graduated this last summer. Dr. Chris Miele received his Ph.D. with a dissertation titled "Shearing shelves, mathematical modeling, and fracture factories: analytic and finite element approaches to solving the Stokes equations of glacier flow." Dakota Pyles received his M.S. with a thesis titled "Mass Continuity Constraints on Ice Ablation in Greenland."

Congrats Dakota and Chris!
